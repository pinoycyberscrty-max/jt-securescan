FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an external security audit? +
An external audit checks your public-facing systems, website, and services for vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and exposure risks.
Do you need access to my passwords or backend? +
No. External audits are performed from the outside — the same way attackers would see your system. No backend access required.
How long does an audit take? +
Most audits take 24–48 hours, depending on complexity. You'll get your full report and a walkthrough call within this timeframe.
Will the audit affect my website or system? +
No. All scans are safe, read-only, and non-intrusive — they're designed not to disrupt your services or modify any data.
Do you provide a report? +
Yes. Every audit includes a structured report with findings, severity ratings, and clear step-by-step remediation guidance for your team.
Will you help me fix the issues? +
I provide detailed guidance and implementation steps for every finding. Your team implements the fixes, and I'm available to answer questions and clarify remediation steps.
